Good bones — reveal is coherent and accessible, orb/packet add narrative without theft. But high-frequency hovers at 700ms feel sluggish (Emil), three infinite pulses draw attention with no pause, and the two main content swaps (hero tab, gallery) plus the lightbox have no crafted exit — they just appear. Emil Kowalski — Restraint & Speed

Selective
Problem — 700ms on every hover is 3× the budget for triggers users hit dozens of times per visit.

What's working well

  • ✓ Keyboard-initiated gallery arrows correctly do not add extra flourish — they reuse the same crossfade, not a new bounce.
  • ✓ No scale(0) entrances; smallest is scale(.98) on swaps.
  • ✓ Custom ease cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1) (iOS sheet curve) used consistently instead of ease.

Issues to address

CriticalEmil

700ms hovers on high-frequency controls — nav, hero tabs, gallery

WhatEvery interactive at docs/index.html:92 bears transition-all duration-700 — nav links, hero tabs, gallery arrows, CTA hovers. Users retarget mid-flight and feel lag.

Why it mattersFrequency gate: nav/tabs are frequent (tens/visit). Emil: under 300ms, ideal 180ms. 700ms + transform on hover is interruptible but still sluggish; keyframes can't retarget is not the issue — duration is.

Recommended motionHover 150–200ms ease-out, active scale(.97) 120ms. Retain 700ms only for orb background (rare, ambient). Switch hovers to transition: background 180ms, transform 180ms with interruptible transitions.
